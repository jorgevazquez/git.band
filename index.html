About

git.band is a four-piece industrial metal project forged in the machine rooms of the American rust belt. Drawing from the sounds of hydraulic press lines, arc welding, and blast furnace cycles, the band translates the raw violence of industrial labor into structured noise. No samples. No triggers. Pure signal chain brutality.

  • Karev // Vocals & Electronics
  • Solt // Guitar & Noise
  • Wen // Bass & Synthesis
  • Drave // Drums & Percussion
